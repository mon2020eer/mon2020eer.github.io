Who we are

The story

One idea, born from something everyone who has traveled from Tripoli knows.

It started with something everyone who has traveled from Tripoli knows: one trip is bought from four different places.

The ticket from one office, the hotel from a website that won't take your card, the visa file from someone who knows the process, the airport pickup from a number a friend gave you. Four parties, four prices, and four times explaining your story from the start. And when a flight is delayed or a file gets stuck, you don't know who to call, because each party only handles its own piece.

Almasar Almuttahid Tourism Services was founded to close that gap. One party handles the whole trip, from the first question to the moment you're home. One price, told to you before you pay, not after. And a booking reference you can check yourself with the airline or hotel, because trust is built on what can be verified, not on what is said.

We're at the start of the road and we don't hide it. A new company has no archive of stories, but it has something heavier: every client in our first year is the difference between growing and not. That's a stronger commitment than any track record.

We work with individuals, families and private companies, and we don't promise what we can't deliver. We promise the route stays one, and that the person who answers you is the same person who started with you.
